## Patterns
- Where the model is wrong (12 analyses): (a) it copies the written contrast or footer order instead of the stated result: SCF8 (4 analyses, 13 cells), BIID group x ownership (cZqU), and the 4-condition interaction in 52TV; in UqKD it contradicts its own definition. (b) it defaults to a positive correlation when the sign is weak or hidden in a shifted table: e3e7 x3 (read P values as r), wubM (r = -0.07 written as +), 4n8Mg 041b. (c) increase/decrease with a footer inequality that disagrees with the label (SCF8).
- Convention conflicts (8 analyses) are Astra and the model each using a different, internally consistent rule: contrast weights as written (Astra in DDEZ, sZB8, 6XNY) vs sign of the effect (model); effect sign (Astra) vs label (model) in VJVb. Astra is not consistent across papers, so these are a spec problem for the direction field, not model errors.
- Scorer artifacts (6 analyses, 10 cells, 21% of the flagged cells): name-similarity pairing of two analyses on one key crosses them (7iEd, yHKi); condition links of a model level ("non-hostile" = NJ+NS) or a near-duplicate pair (NC/NO) are mapped to the wrong Astra level (RDqb, kPpi).
- Null/weak correlations (e3e7, wubM, UfR, cf54) account for 7 of the reversed cells but carry no finding.

## Caveats
- One seed, one fine-tuned model (one checkpoint), the 17 validation papers that contain flips; all 27 analyses judged, nothing sampled. The 4,353 denominator is the number both sides wrote as given; I did not recompute it.
- Judgments rest on the pubget text.txt; figures were not read. Tables with the shift problem: only e3e7 Table 2 (shifted); tables for 52TV, 7iEd, UqKD, rTBy checked against their headers and not shifted.
- Astra is not ground truth: 0/12 decided is the model against Astra only where the paper decides; the 8 both_acceptable and 1 unclear cases are left out of that figure. The SCF8 footer ambiguity moves 3 analyses [10 cells] between astra_right and both_acceptable.
- "no_reversal" is my addition to the requested labels; if it is counted as model-not-wrong, the shares above hold; counted as a scorer problem, the dirflip method over-reports by 10 cells.
- The model's cells in the no_reversal analyses can still be wrong for other reasons (7iEd: model wrote a different ROI than gold; RDqb: it coded a hostile level); only the reversal is not real.
